### Hey, I'm Anuraag 👋

I lead product for Subscriptions, Loyalty and AI at [Delivery Hero / Foodpanda](https://www.deliveryhero.com), based in Singapore. I started out as an architect, co-founded two startups, and I've never really lost the habit of making things myself.

That's what this page is for. I build to learn. Most of what's here started as a small annoyance or a question I couldn't stop thinking about, and building the thing myself is how I figure out what's actually worth building.

### Things I've been building 🛠️

- 🎙️ **[LocalFlow](https://github.com/anuraagburman/localflow)**: I wanted clean, smart dictation without paying $15 a month or sending my voice to the cloud. So I made one that runs entirely on my Mac.
- 🧰 **[Personal Assistant Toolkit](https://github.com/anuraagburman/personal-assistant-toolkit)**: I got tired of copy-pasting my calendar and inbox into Claude. This MCP server gives it the context directly and turns it into one morning briefing.
- 🫀 **[Baseline](https://github.com/anuraagburman/baseline)**: My watch gives me dozens of numbers a day and almost no advice. Baseline turns that data into one plain decision a day. Still in progress.
- 📈 **[PortfolioIQ](https://github.com/anuraagburman/portfolioiq)**: Retail investors get institutional markets without institutional tools. This was my attempt at closing the gap, built end-to-end over a weekend for about $5.
- 🔎 **[Market Sentinel](https://github.com/anuraagburman/market-sentinel)**: A research assistant for US equities that shows its evidence before it offers an opinion. For research and paper decisions only.

At work, I've also shipped a RAG knowledge base for our support teams and an agentic pipeline that triages tickets in under three seconds. Those are internal, but I wrote them up on my site.
